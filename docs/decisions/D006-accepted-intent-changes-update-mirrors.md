# D006: Accepting an intent change updates every place that wording appears

Status: accepted by the user, 23 November 2026.

## Principle

The problem, users and offering each appear in more than one place in the
sponsor's workspace: the intent field, the Business Intent framing and summary,
and the linked Context answer. When the sponsor accepts a change from
Decisions & issues, those places must not drift apart.

## Rules

1. Accepting a change to the problem, users or offering updates the intent field,
   its linked Context answer, and the framing statement or context in the same
   private draft.
2. Summary lines (problem, offering) move with it when they still match the
   earlier wording. If the sponsor rewrote a summary line differently, it is left
   as written and the response names it for review.
3. Everything stays in the private draft until the sponsor shares for direction
   review and publishes. Earlier versions are preserved.
