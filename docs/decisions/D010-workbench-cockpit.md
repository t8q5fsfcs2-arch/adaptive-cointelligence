# D010: The workbench as a cockpit

Status: accepted by the user, 24 November 2026. Built on `claude/thesis-board`.

## Principle

In a team's private workbench, the live conversation and working draft stay
dominant. Next moves and every tool for thought are one click away at the edge,
and open beside the conversation, never in its place.

## What it is

1. **Heads-up strip** under the page heading: the next step in your own work,
   "inputs changed" when the basis moved, the weakest requirement area on your
   team's side with its next step, and the question your team is validating.
   "Review work" sits at its end.
2. **Instrument rail** at the right edge (a bottom bar on small screens): Next
   moves, Reviews, then Brief, Building blocks or Alignment, User flows, Prototype
   alternatives (Design), Outcomes report and Updates, with counts. It replaces
   the former tab row, "Next:" button and Updates link; every earlier action is
   still there.
3. **Next moves** lists, in order: inputs that changed, what other people need
   from you (the same items as Project home), the next step in your own work, and
   up to two areas where your team's work would count most.
4. **Reviews** lists the saved AI partner reviews of this work. A review still
   runs only when the person asks for one.

## Boundaries

Presentation only. Every item is read from existing records and state; nothing new
is stored and no command changed. The cockpit does not generate AI output.
Unprompted AI partner suggestions inside the conversation, as drawn on the design
canvas, are not built: that is a new agent behaviour and needs its own decision
(trigger, contract, limits) and a live model connection. The app's main
navigation is unchanged.
