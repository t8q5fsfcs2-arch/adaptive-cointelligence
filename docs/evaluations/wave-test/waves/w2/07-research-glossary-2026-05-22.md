# Research team glossary (excerpt), 22 May 2026. Maintained by Sam Patel.

**Rescheduling barrier.** A structural obstacle outside the product that stops a patient from changing a visit they intend to keep in some form: office hours, phone queues, staffing.
Mapped to enterprise concept **Access barrier** ("a structural obstacle preventing a person from obtaining a service they intend to use") as: same as. Adopted by the Research lead.
