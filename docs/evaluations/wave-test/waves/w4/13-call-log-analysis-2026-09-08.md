# After-hours call-log analysis
8 September 2026. Sam Patel with Operations Analytics.

- 85% of after-hours reschedule attempts fall between 5 pm and 9 pm. Very few after 9 pm.
- In follow-up calls, 10 of 12 original interviewees said a staff callback the next morning would be acceptable if they could request it in the evening.

Revised reading: round-the-clock self-service is not required. Self-service until 9 pm, plus a next-morning callback request, meets the need. I've revised R3 accordingly (9 Sept): "Rescheduling works until 9 pm without staff; after that, patients can request a next-morning callback."
