# Riverside Clinics: Fewer Missed Appointments
Kickoff deck, 2 March 2026. Dana Ortiz, Director of Operations (sponsor and decision owner).

- 18% of booked visits are missed across our four clinics.
- Goal: automated reminders will cut missed visits from 18% to 10% within six months of launch.
- Patients forget. A reminder 48 hours ahead fixes most of it.
- Team: Sam Patel (Research lead), Lee Chen (Design lead), Ravi Gomez (Engineering lead), Ana Brooks (clinic manager, Northside; contributor).
