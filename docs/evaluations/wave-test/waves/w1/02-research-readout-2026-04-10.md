# Research readout: why visits are missed
10 April 2026. Sam Patel (Research).

**Method.** 12 interviews. Participants were recruited from the evening-clinic list at Northside and Eastgate; all 12 work shifts or irregular hours. Synthetic study.

**Main finding.** Most people who missed a visit already knew in advance that they couldn't attend (9 of 12). Forgetting was the reason for 2. The barrier is rescheduling, not remembering.

Example, P07 (warehouse shift worker): "I knew on Tuesday I couldn't come. The office only answers nine to five."

Confidence: moderate. Applies to: shift and irregular-hours workers. Wider population not yet studied.
